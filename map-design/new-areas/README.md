# New areas and details: what to put here

The official board gives the whole campus layout, so nothing big is missing any more. What helps now is detail:
how each building really looks, and what is inside the rooms you want to walk into.

Make one folder per building or place, for example `new-areas/3rd-academic/` or `new-areas/female-hall/`. Anything
helps; nothing here is required. Inside each folder:

1. **Photos** taken from the ground: the fronts (especially the south side, the side the game camera sees),
   entrances, roof edges, signs. Inside photos for rooms you want enterable. Arrows or circles drawn on them are
   welcome.
2. **A short note** (`notes.md` or a text file) about what makes the place feel like IUT.
3. **Story ideas** (optional): what should happen there.

**Most wanted**, because these were built from the aerial photos alone:

- the 3rd Academic Building (#18)
- the three workshops (#15–17)
- the Electrical Substation (#19)
- the VC Residence (#20)
- the Female Hall (#21)
- the F Type and D Type staff quarters (#22, #23)
- the Medical Center / Laundry (#24)
- the New Male Hall site (#25)
- **one photo of the campus board's north-west and south-east corners**. Your photo cuts them off; they hold the
  rest of #24 and the Main Gate.

Template for `notes.md`:

```
Building / place:
Board number (1-27), if any:
What it looks like (materials, storeys, colours, roof):
Inside (if it should be enterable):
Entrances (which side):
Anything special (trees, signs, people's habits at night):
Story ideas:
```

Then tell me the folder is ready. I'll rebuild that part the same way as the rest of the campus and update the map.
